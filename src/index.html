Ability Arena - Game Rules

Game Overview

Ability Arena is a turn-based strategy game played on a 7×7 grid. Players select special abilities and engage in tactical combat with the goal of eliminating their opponent by reducing their health to zero or pushing them off the board.

Setup

  • Each player secretly chooses two abilities from the available list (these will be revealed only after both players have made their selections)
  • Player 1 starts on the center square of the first row
  • Player 2 starts on the center square of the seventh row
  • Each player begins with 100 health points
  • Each player has a total of 5 minutes thinking time for the entire game

Gameplay

On their turn, a player MUST choose ONE of the following actions:

  • Move their piece one square (orthogonally, or diagonally if they have the Diagonal Moving ability)
  • Use one of their chosen abilities

Exception: If a player just placed a Block on their previous turn, they may choose to pass their turn without moving or using an ability.

Abilities

  • Bow and Arrow: Deals 35 damage to opponent if they are within 2 squares (non-diagonal). Unlimited uses.
  • Cross Bow: Deals 50 damage to opponent if they are within 3 squares (non-diagonal). Unlocked when reaching the final rank. Unlimited uses.
  • Sword: Deals 50 damage to opponent if they are in an adjacent square (non-diagonal). Unlimited uses.
  • Shield: Completely blocks any damage when activated. Can be used during the opponent's turn in response to an attack. Limited to 3 uses total throughout the game.
  • Push: Pushes opponent 3 squares in a straight line. If this pushes them off the board, they are eliminated. Unlimited uses.
  • Diagonal Moving: Passive ability that allows diagonal movement. Unlimited uses.
  • Turret: Places a permanent turret on any empty square. The turret deals 50 damage to any player who moves into any square adjacent to it. Cannot be used on the first turn. Limited to 1 use total.
  • Walls: Places a wall between two adjacent squares. Walls prevent movement between those squares. Limited to 6 uses total.
  • Blocks: Places a block on any empty square. Blocks make the square impassable. Limited to 5 uses total.

Winning the Game

A player wins by:

  1. Reducing their opponent's health points to 0 or below.
  2. Pushing their opponent off the board.
  3. If their opponent runs out of their 5-minute time allocation.
